Terms of Service

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Version 1.0

1. About This Application

Touchline Studio ("the App") is a private, locally-hosted content management tool designed for a single operator to create, compose, and publish sports-related social media content during major football tournaments. The App is not available to the public and is not offered as a commercial service.

2. Permitted Use

The App is operated solely by its owner for personal content creation purposes. It is not used to access, collect, or process data belonging to any third party. All publishing activity is directed exclusively at social media accounts owned and operated by the App's owner.

3. Third-Party Platform Integrations

The App integrates with third-party platforms including TikTok, Meta (Instagram and Facebook), Google (YouTube), and others using their official published APIs. All integrations are implemented strictly in accordance with each platform's Developer Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

The App does not circumvent, bypass, or abuse any platform's rate limits, authentication systems, or content policies. API credentials are stored locally on the operator's device and are never shared with third parties.

4. Content Responsibility

The operator is solely responsible for all content created and published through the App. The App does not generate, moderate, or distribute content autonomously. All posts are reviewed and approved by the operator before publishing.

5. No Warranty

The App is provided for personal use without warranty of any kind. The operator assumes all responsibility for its operation and use.

6. Changes

These terms may be updated at any time. The most current version will always be published at this URL.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Version 1.0

1. Overview

Touchline Studio is a single-operator, locally-hosted desktop application. This policy explains how the App handles data in the context of its integrations with third-party social media platforms, including TikTok.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Touchline Studio does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data from any individual other than its owner. The App has no user accounts, no analytics tracking, no advertising systems, and no remote telemetry of any kind.

3. Local Data Storage

All application data — including API access tokens, post drafts, media files, and configuration settings — is stored exclusively on the operator's local device in a SQLite database and local file system. No application data is transmitted to or stored on any server operated by Touchline Studio.

4. TikTok Platform Data

In connection with TikTok API usage, the App accesses only the operator's own TikTok account for the sole purpose of publishing video content created by the operator. The App requests the minimum scopes required: video.upload and video.publish.

No TikTok user data belonging to third parties is accessed, requested, collected, or stored at any time. The App does not access follower data, engagement metrics, direct messages, or any other account data beyond what is strictly necessary to submit a video for publishing.

TikTok access tokens are stored locally on the operator's device and are never transmitted to any third-party server.

5. Other Platform Integrations

The same principles apply to all other platform integrations (Meta, Google/YouTube). Only the operator's own accounts are accessed. Only publishing-related scopes are requested. No third-party user data is collected or stored.

6. Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally, the operator controls all data retention. Uninstalling the App or deleting the application folder removes all locally stored data.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is operated by an adult and is not directed at children. No data relating to minors is collected or processed.

8. Contact

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact the App's owner through their registered TikTok developer account.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. The most current version will always be published at this URL.
